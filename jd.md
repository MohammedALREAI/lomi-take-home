# Senior Full Stack Engineer — Lomi Labs

**Location:** Remote · **Type:** Full-time · **Compensation:** $3,000–$4,500/month USD

## About us

Lomi Labs is the product engineering arm of the team behind **Lomi**. We run many projects in parallel, each with real users, real deadlines, and real production stakes. You'll be working across our client and lab projects — often as the engineer who owns a project end to end.

## The role

We're looking for a **senior full stack engineer** who can take a project from a loose requirement to a running system: design the data model, build the API and the frontend, wire up the infrastructure, ship it, and keep it healthy. On any given month you might be building a Next.js dashboard for one client, a document-processing worker for another, and reviewing a teammate's Terraform for a third.

## What you'll do

- Own full-stack delivery on client projects: architecture, data modeling, API design, frontend, deployment.
- Build and maintain web apps in **React/Next.js** and backends in **Node.js** (Express/Nest or similar), in **TypeScript**, usually inside **Turborepo-style monorepos**.
- Design schemas and queries in **Postgres** (we use Prisma heavily), with **Redis** for caching/queues and occasionally **MongoDB or Firebase** where a project calls for it.
- Deploy and operate services on **GCP and AWS** (Cloud Run, Cloud Build, ECS/Lambda, Secret Manager), with infrastructure as code (**Terraform**).
- Build and ship with **Docker** daily — local dev stacks, CI, and production images.
- Integrate third-party and AI services: **LLM APIs** (Anthropic, OpenAI), Stripe, auth providers (e.g. Clerk), webhooks.
- Move between projects without losing the thread: read an unfamiliar codebase fast, respect its conventions, and leave it better documented than you found it.

## What we're looking for

- Deep, hands-on **TypeScript/Node.js** experience on the backend (Express, Nest, or similar) and **React** on the frontend. Next.js experience is a strong plus.
- Solid **relational database** skills: schema design, migrations, query performance in Postgres (or equivalent). Comfort with at least one NoSQL store (MongoDB, Firestore, Redis-as-datastore).
- Production experience on **AWS or GCP**, including deploying, monitoring, and debugging live services. Terraform or comparable IaC experience.
- Real **Docker** experience: writing Dockerfiles, Compose-based local dev environments, and debugging containers in CI and production.
- Hands-on experience building on **LLM APIs** (Anthropic, OpenAI, or similar): tool use, structured extraction, prompt design, and keeping an eye on cost and correctness.
- Comfort in **monorepos** — workspace tooling (pnpm/yarn/turbo), shared packages, keeping boundaries clean.
- Strong fundamentals that transfer: HTTP, auth, caching, queues, background jobs, idempotency, testing.
- The agency temperament: you're energized (not drained) by switching contexts, you make sensible decisions with incomplete information, and you communicate clearly in writing.

## Nice to have

- Python — several of our projects pair a TS web app with Python workers/pipelines.
- CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, Cloud Build).
- Experience with document processing (PDF parsing, OCR) or data-heavy backends.
- Prior agency/startup experience, or having been the early engineer somewhere.

## How we work

- Small senior team, high trust, low ceremony. Code review and written design notes over meetings.
- Every project keeps a living source of truth for decisions and open work — we write things down.
- AI-assisted development is part of how we build; you should be comfortable using these tools critically, and your fundamentals need to be strong enough to catch what they get wrong.
- We ship behind a human check: in client work, correctness and provenance beat speed.

## Hiring process

1. **Apply** — send us your CV/portfolio and a short note on the most interesting system you've built.
2. **Shortlist** — we review each application and shortlist the candidates whose experience fits the role.
3. **Take-home assignment** — shortlisted candidates receive a realistic, scoped assignment on a stack like ours.
4. **Interviews** — candidates whose assignment stands out are invited to interviews, where we dig into the assignment, their past work, and how they think.
5. **Offer** — after the interviews, we make an offer to the candidate we want to work with.
